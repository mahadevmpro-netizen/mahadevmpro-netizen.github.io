NORDHAVEN: urban infrastructure mapping and geospatial data engineering

An end-to-end mapping workflow on open data in Bremen, Germany. Road areas, assets, curbs, drainage channels and traffic signs are digitised into a structured GeoPackage, given a consistent attribute schema, checked, and finished as a print-ready map.

Independent demonstration project. It is not client work or an official inventory.

  • QGIS 3.10
  • GeoPackage
  • EPSG:25832
  • Attribute schema
  • QA/QC
  • Cartography

About

I am a civil engineer who moved into geospatial data work. For about three years I have produced and quality-checked infrastructure data: mapping road areas, point assets, linear features and traffic signs, and making sure the result is consistent before it is handed over.

I care about the parts that make data usable: the right geometry type for each feature, a clear attribute structure, and checks you can describe honestly, including what they do not cover.

Based in Bengaluru, India. B.Tech in Civil Engineering, 2022.
